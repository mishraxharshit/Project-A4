# Governance

This document describes how Project-A4 is currently governed. As the community grows, this structure is expected to evolve — proposed changes to governance should go through the standard contribution process.

## Roles

### Maintainers

Maintainers have merge rights and are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Triaging issues
- Upholding the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- Setting technical and research direction in collaboration with the community

Current maintainers are listed in [`CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).

### Contributors

Anyone who submits a pull request, issue, or research contribution that is accepted into the project.

## Decision-Making

- **Day-to-day changes** (bug fixes, small features, documentation) are approved by any one maintainer via standard PR review.
- **Significant changes** (new sub-projects, breaking API changes, major research direction shifts, governance changes) require:
  1. An issue or discussion proposing the change, open for community comment.
  2. Sign-off from the project's maintainers.
- Disagreements are resolved by discussion first; if consensus cannot be reached, the founding maintainer(s) make the final call, with reasoning documented publicly.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who consistently provide high-quality contributions and demonstrate good judgment in reviews may be nominated by an existing maintainer. Nominations are discussed among current maintainers before being finalized.

## Sub-Projects

As the ecosystem grows, sub-projects may be split into their own repositories. Each sub-project may define its own maintainers, subject to alignment with this document and the overall Code of Conduct.

## Changes to This Document

Changes to governance are proposed via pull request and require maintainer consensus before merge.
